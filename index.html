Theodoridis records TWO versions: 'Apolytikion from Ga' (track 2, diatonic) and 'Apolytikion from Zo' (track 3, enharmonic). This card describes the Ga version. The Zo version drones on low Zo until the final cadence, then drops 3 semis (toward Di) under the closing melisma. IMPORTANT: the purchased Grave album is the ENGLISH edition (language auto-detected 2026-07-22); the Greek edition is a separate album with different track lengths. Compare both tracks, it is the clearest base-note contrast in the whole set.
